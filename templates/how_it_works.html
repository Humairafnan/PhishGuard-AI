How PhishGuard AI Works

An overview of the hybrid deep learning architecture, risk analysis, and explainability framework.

Detection Pipeline

1

Email Input

The user provides an email message for analysis.

↓

2

Text Representation

The email text is tokenized and processed before entering the model.

↓

3

ELECTRA Encoder

The ELECTRA transformer extracts contextual language representations.

↓

4

CNN Feature Extraction

CNN layers capture important local patterns and phishing-related phrases.

↓

5

Classification

The hybrid model predicts whether an email is legitimate or phishing.

Additional Security Analysis

🌐 URL Security Analysis

URLs inside emails are evaluated using security rules including suspicious domains, keywords, and URL patterns.

📊 Confidence Assessment

Prediction confidence is converted into understandable risk categories such as legitimate, uncertain, and phishing risk.

🔍 Explainable AI

Integrated Gradients highlights important tokens that influenced the model decision.

Why Explainable AI?

Deep learning models can achieve high accuracy but are often difficult to interpret. PhishGuard AI uses Integrated Gradients to provide transparent explanations by identifying words that increase or decrease the phishing prediction score.
